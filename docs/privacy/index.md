Nearo: Bluetooth Finder ("Nearo", "the app") is made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

## The short version

- Nearo finds Bluetooth devices near you. **Scan results stay on your phone.** We never send them to us or to anyone else. Scan results include device names, addresses, signal strength, makers and the list of paired devices.
- There is no account and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
- Devices you save, last-seen records, protection mode settings and tracker records also stay on your phone.
- A place is recorded only if you turn that on yourself, and then only to about 100 m.
- The free version shows ads. To do that we use Google services. They receive limited technical data, described below, and nothing about the devices around you.

## Data that stays on your device

- **Bluetooth scan results and your paired-device list.** Used only to show nearby devices, estimate distance and direction, and remember the devices you hide. Stored only in the app's local storage.
- **Motion and compass sensor readings.** Used in real time to point the arrow and to tell whether the phone is moving. They are not stored.
- **Saved devices.** When you save a device to My devices, its address, the name and signal details it broadcasts, and the name and type you give it are stored only on this phone. Removing the device from the list deletes them.
- **Last seen time and place (place is optional).** For your own paired or saved devices, Nearo remembers when this phone last detected them. The place is recorded only after you turn on "Record last location", in a device's last-seen card or in Settings › Privacy. It is your phone's approximate position at that moment, rounded to about 100 m, and it is not where the device is now. You can turn recording off at any time and delete the stored places. "Open in Maps" hands only the coordinates to the maps app you choose.
- **Protection mode (optional).** While you have it on, Nearo keeps running with a visible notification and listens for the Bluetooth signals of the devices you chose, to tell you when one may have been left behind. The list of watched devices, mutes and current alerts are stored on this phone. It does not start by itself after the phone restarts, and it does not use the background location permission.
- **Unknown tracker detection (optional, part of protection mode).** Nearo listens for tracker-type Bluetooth signals and uses the motion sensor only to tell whether the phone is moving. When a tracker that is not yours seems to move with you, its Bluetooth address and the times it was first and last detected are stored on this phone for at most 48 hours. If you mark a tracker as known, its address is kept on this phone so you are not alerted again. No location and no step count is used.
- **Your premium purchase status and 24-hour pass.** Stored on the device. Google Play processes the payment itself. A 24-hour pass earned by watching an ad is recorded only as a start time on the device.

None of the above is uploaded. Saved devices, hidden devices, last-seen records, protection mode data and tracker records are also excluded from Android backup and device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything.

## Data collected by third-party services

| Service | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob | Advertising ID, app set ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions, including whether an optional rewarded ad was watched to the end | To show and measure ads (banner, full-screen and optional rewarded ads) and to prevent fraud |
| Google Firebase Analytics | Aggregate app events, such as "a search started", "found within 1 minute" or "a device was saved" (a count only), plus device type, OS version, app version, language, country and installation ID | To understand which features work and improve the app |
| Google Firebase Crashlytics | Crash reports: stack traces, device model, OS version and installation ID | To fix crashes |
| Google Play Billing | Purchase information, handled by Google | To process the one-time premium purchase |

None of these services receive your Bluetooth scan results, your saved devices, the names you give them, recorded places, protection mode data or tracker records. Ad requests carry no information about the devices around you. Their own privacy policies apply: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Permissions

- **Nearby devices (Bluetooth):** to scan for devices and to show the devices paired with this phone.
- **Location:** Android 11 and earlier require it for Bluetooth scanning. On every Android version, your approximate location is read only if you turn on "Record last location", and only to save the place described above on this phone. Nearo never asks for background location.
- **Notifications:** for the protection mode status notification and its alerts.
- **Foreground service (connected device, location):** so protection mode can keep listening while the screen is off, always with a visible notification.
- **Run at startup:** only to tell you that protection mode is off after the phone restarts. Nothing is started.
- **Vibration:** for haptic feedback while finding.

## Ads and your choices

- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and usage analytics and crash reports stay off unless you agree to storage on your device. You can change your choice at any time in the app's settings (**Settings › Privacy options**).
- Rewarded ads are optional. You choose to watch one in exchange for 24 hours of Premium, at most once a day. Full-screen ads appear only after you finish finding a device, never while you are searching.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android **Settings › Google › Ads** (the wording varies by device).
- Buying Nearo Premium removes ads. So does an active 24-hour pass.

## Children

Nearo is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Data retention and deletion

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. On your phone, tracker records are deleted after at most 48 hours, a last-seen record is deleted when its device is no longer one of yours, and everything else stays until you remove it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google under its retention settings: 14 months for Analytics and 90 days for Crashlytics.

## Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit.

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
